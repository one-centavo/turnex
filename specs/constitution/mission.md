# Mission

Defines the core purpose of TURNEX. It serves as the primary benchmark for deciding whether a feature or requirement belongs in the product.

## What We Are Building

TURNEX is a multi-business web platform focused on virtual queue management, real-time ticket tracking, and digital catalogs of products and services, designed to optimize wait times and eliminate physical crowding at commercial venues.

### Core Product Pillars

1. **Customer Showcase, Ordering & Digital Ticket** — Enables customers to explore the business catalog of products and services, place orders, process payments, receive a digital queue ticket, and monitor real-time progress until order pick-up or service dispatch.
2. **Business Operations Dashboard** — Allows merchants to receive incoming order notifications, manage product order preparation or call tickets to service modules, controlling queue flow in real time.
3. **SuperAdmin Management Portal** — Manages platform governance, reviewing and approving merchant legal and banking documentation (KYB) before enabling public operations, in addition to overseeing global categories and platform metrics.

## Target Audience

- **End Customer:** Seeks to save time, purchase conveniently, and wait for their turn without physical lines or wait-time uncertainty.
- **Businesses & Merchants (Services & Products):** Seek to streamline their in-person service flow, minimize counter congestion, and process paid orders efficiently and methodically.
- **SuperAdmin & Platform Operators:** Seek to ensure merchant legitimacy via KYB compliance and maintain a trusted, secure commercial ecosystem.

## Core Principles

- **Customer Autonomy (Total Self-Service)** — Customers independently manage their entire journey (browse, order, pay, and track their ticket) from their own device without unnecessary friction or human gatekeepers.
- **Multi-Business Flexibility** — The platform accommodates businesses offering physical products for in-store pickup as well as walk-in services with or without upfront payment.
- **Immediate Cloud Scalability** — A lightweight, resilient, and responsive web architecture built to absorb concurrency surges and peak-hour traffic spikes at commercial venues.
- **Strict Transaction Security & Legitimacy** — Fraud prevention and data protection through progressive user verification (OTP) and rigorous merchant compliance verification (KYB) before live financial processing.

## What It Is NOT

- **Not a food delivery or last-mile courier platform** — It does not manage delivery driver fleets, route dispatching, or home delivery; its scope is strictly store pick-up or in-person service.
- **Not a comprehensive ERP or accounting suite** — It does not handle raw material inventory tracking, employee payroll, or corporate tax accounting.
- **Not a mandatory native application** — It is a 100% web-based solution optimized for mobile browsers, requiring no App Store or Play Store downloads from customers or merchants.
